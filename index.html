Ingredients & Preparation

🌶️ Fresh Ingredients

  • Fresh Green Chillies (Slit) 250g
  • Yellow & Black Mustard Seeds 3 tbsp
  • Fennel Seeds (Saunf) 1.5 tbsp
  • Fenugreek Seeds (Methi) 1/2 tsp
  • Pure Turmeric & Hing 1 tsp
  • Rock Salt / Sea Salt 2 tbsp
  • Fresh Lemon Juice 3 tbsp
  • Cold-Pressed Mustard / Sesame Oil 4 tbsp

🥣 How We Make It (Step-by-Step)

  • Washing & Drying: Fresh chillies are washed and dried thoroughly with a cotton cloth until zero moisture remains.
  • Slitting: Chillies are slit vertically to allow the spice blend to infuse deeply.
  • Roasting & Grinding: Mustard, fennel, and fenugreek seeds are gently roasted and coarsely ground.
  • Tossing & Marination: The chillies are tossed with spices, lemon juice, turmeric, salt, and warmed oil.
  • Sun Curing: Transferred to sterilized glass jars and sun-cured for 3 days to achieve the magical bite!
