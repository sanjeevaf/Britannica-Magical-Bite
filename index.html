Preparation & Authentic Ingredients

Pure & Natural Ingredients

  • Fresh Green Chillies: Hand-picked, thick, and crisp chillies.
  • Spices: Coarsely ground mustard seeds (Rai), fennel seeds (Saunf), and fenugreek (Methi).
  • Seasoning: Pure rock salt, turmeric powder, and asafoetida (Hing).
  • Tanginess: Fresh squeezed lemon juice.
  • Oil: Cold-pressed mustard / sesame oil.

Our Step-by-Step Recipe

  • Washing & Sun-Drying: Chillies are thoroughly washed, wiped completely dry with a clean cloth, and air-dried to remove all moisture.
  • Slitting: Each green chilli is slit lengthwise or chopped into bite-sized pieces.
  • Spice Blend: Freshly roasted mustard, fennel, salt, and turmeric are mixed with warm oil and lemon extract.
  • Marination: Chillies are tossed into the spice blend and left in sterile glass jars for 2 to 3 days to develop peak flavor.
